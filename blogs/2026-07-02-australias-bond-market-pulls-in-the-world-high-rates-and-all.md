Australia's bond market is experiencing significant growth, with issuance up 42% in the first half of 2026 to A$191.7 billion, attracting international borrowers despite elevated interest rates. The market's expansion reflects its evolution into a core global funding destination, supported by Australia's strong fundamentals, quality investor base, and deepening liquidity infrastructure.
