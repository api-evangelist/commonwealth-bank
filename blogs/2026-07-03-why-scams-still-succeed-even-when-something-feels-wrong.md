CommBank research reveals that nearly one in three scam victims suspected something was amiss but proceeded anyway, demonstrating how modern scams exploit decision-making psychology through urgency and pressure. The study emphasizes that awareness alone doesn't prevent fraud; instead, victims must act on their instincts by verifying information and consulting trusted contacts before engaging with suspicious requests.
